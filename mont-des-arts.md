# Mont des Arts content and source note

Reviewed 10 November 2026. Mont des Arts (Kunstberg) is a cultural district and garden in central Brussels. The garden lies below Place Royale between the Royal Quarter and the lower historic centre. This repository serves the independent visitor guide at https://montdesarts.org/; it does not represent a public authority or the surrounding cultural institutions. Keep the existing sitewide disclosure.
